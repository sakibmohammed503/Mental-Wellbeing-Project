Why Digital Well-Being Matters More Than Ever

Mental health is a fundamental human right. Recent behavioral research reveals that passive short-form video consumption and late-night blue light exposure alter dopamine baseline response and fragment sleep architecture.

The Digital Addiction Feedback Loop

High pickup frequency and continuous context-switching elevate cortisol levels, exacerbating fatigue and reducing cognitive stamina. On the other hand, structured screen curfews, consistent sleep hygiene, and intentional offline breaks build resilience and restore natural executive function.
